CONCLUSION
=================================================================
Naive Bayes Only achieved the highest F1-score of the three approaches
this run. The Hybrid model still achieves the highest precision
(90.65%), trading a small amount of recall/F1 for
substantially fewer false positives -- see prediction-time heuristics
in predict_hybrid() (English-language filter, self-directed-distress
dampening) for what drives that trade-off.

Formula : R_score = 0.6 x NB_probability + 0.4 x VADER_score
Decision (NB / VADER) : R_score >= 0.5 -> AGGRESSIVE, R_score < 0.5 -> SAFE
